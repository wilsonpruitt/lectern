# Call to Worship — Proper 23 (28), Year A

**Season after Pentecost** · complementary track
Readings: Isaiah 25:1-9 · Psalm 23 · Philippians 4:1-9 · Matthew 22:1-14

The complementary track converges on one image — **the banquet**: the feast on
the mountain (Isa 25), the table prepared (Ps 23), the wedding hall thrown open
to the streets (Matt 22).

---

Come to the table the Lord has spread.
**The feast is ready, and the doors are open.**

On this mountain God lays out rich food for all peoples.
**Here our cups overflow.**

We came hungry, and grief came with us.
**God will swallow up death and wipe every tear away.**

The invitation has gone out into the streets.
**We heard it, and we have come.**

Come, let us worship the God who sets the table.
**We come to the feast. Amen.**

---

*Alternate (semicontinuous track — Exodus 32 golden calf / Psalm 106): a call on
mercy-after-rebellion rather than the banquet. Drafted on request; the
banquet track is the stronger Sunday.*
